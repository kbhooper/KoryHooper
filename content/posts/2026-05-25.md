Most of the time driving sucks, especially in large cities. One of our favorite things to do locally is take the train down to Chicago, then get around there on the city trains. It's the closest city with an actual functioning transit system. There's something really awesome about trains, I've become somewhat obsessed with them. My early 20s self with loud exhaust would be dissapointed.
