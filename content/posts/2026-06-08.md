Late May in the Keweenaw peninsula. Temperatures struggled to get above 50 and most of the trees were still bare. This was a camping trip but these pictures are just cool buildings I came across. I'd kill to travel back in time and see some of these towns in early 1900s (obviously before prohibition). 

Think I'll do another post with nature pics. With most of these small but not too small towns there's usually a really cool downtown area with old buildings, etc. Then a couple miles away there's the parkinglot hellscape with Wal-Mart, strip malls, and all that noise. At least they didn't bulldoze this downtown to build a mall #greenbay.
